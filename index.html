Effective date: October 14, 2026  ·  Package: com.storyslicer.app

Short version: StorySlice processes all your videos directly on your device. We never upload your videos, photos, or personal data to any server. The only external data involves ads shown by Google AdMob.

StorySlice does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically:
